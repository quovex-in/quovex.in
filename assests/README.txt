QuoVex editable logo variants
===============================

All files are SVG vector files. They can be imported into Canva and other vector editors.

Variants:
01 Primary Dark
02 Primary Light
03 Horizontal
04 Icon Only
05 Monochrome
06 Gradient
07 Outline
08 Cyan/Dark

In Canva:
Upload -> choose the SVG -> add to design.
You can resize without quality loss. Depending on Canva's SVG handling, individual colors/elements may be editable after import.

Recommended:
- Website light background: 02 or 03
- Website dark background: 01 or 06
- App/avatar/favicon: 04
- Printing/one-color use: 05
- Simple technical brand treatment: 07
